Cocktail OCR Finder

Select your main folder once. Every image in all of its sub-folders is read with OCR and flagged if it shows the word “Cocktail” / “Cocktails” or the names of cocktail drinks. Nothing is uploaded; everything runs in this browser.

Pick the one main folder that holds all your image folders and click Upload. All sub-folders are included automatically.
Your browser may ask “Upload N files to this site?”. That is only a browser permission prompt: the files stay on your computer.
